How FindIt works

Three steps to get your item back

1

Report the item

Create a free account and fill in a short form — item name, category, date, location and a photo. It takes less than a minute.

2

Search & match

Filter by category, location and date, or search by keyword. Every listing shows where and when the item was last seen.

3

Claim & collect

Submit proof of ownership. Once the finder accepts your claim, the item is marked as returned and you can arrange the hand-over.

Found something that isn't yours?

Posting a found item takes a minute and could make someone's day. Your contact details stay hidden until you choose to share them.
