Frequently asked questions

How do I claim an item?

Open the listing and press “Claim this item”. Describe the item and give proof of ownership — serial number, engravings, contents or a receipt. The person who reported it will accept or reject your claim.

Who can see my phone number?

Guests only see a masked version. Logged-in users can unlock your contact details by pressing “Contact reporter”, and you get a notification whenever that happens.

My item is not listed. What now?

Report it as lost with as much detail as possible. You can also check the Lost & Found desk in the Student Affairs office, since valuable items are often handed in there.

Someone posted a fake listing. What should I do?

Use the “Report it” link on the listing. Administrators review every flag and can remove listings and block accounts.

Privacy & safety

  • Contact details are masked for visitors and only unlocked for signed-in users.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes — never in plain text.
  • Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies that expire automatically.
  • Always meet in a public campus location. Never pay money to recover an item.
  • Administrators can remove listings and block accounts that break the rules.
